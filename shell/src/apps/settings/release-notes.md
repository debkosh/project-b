# 0.3.14

- On Android, a download keeps going when you swipe the app away, and opening the app again shows your tabs as they were.
- The Video downloader's Download saves videos from other sites too, straight from the page, and its button turns to a tick once the download starts.
- Downloads from a page's menu, and Retry, now work on sites that only let their own pages download their files.
- A tab group stays when only one of its tabs is left, so the tabs no longer jump as you close them; it ends when its last tab closes.
- A sync link's server address now shows as text, since it can't be changed there.
