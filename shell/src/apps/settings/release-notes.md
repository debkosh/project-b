# 0.3.13

- Settings, Privacy and sites, has Secure DNS: Off, Automatic or Secure only, through Cloudflare, Google, Quad9, CleanBrowsing, NextDNS or a provider of your own.
- Updates and help pages now come from github.com/debkosh/project-b, the project's new home.
- The app now has every resource Chromium's own parts ask for, so none can crash a page for want of one.
- On Android, sites that ask to follow the phone's font size now do, and the app's own pages follow it too; the bars grow up to a third, and the native phone shell's address bar follows it.
- Settings' summaries wrap to a second line rather than being cut short.
- On Android, Install in the update's notification opens the installer again.
- On Android, Relaunch in Settings' Flags starts the app again, where it quit the app, or on Android 10 crashed it.
- On Android, Settings no longer zooms in when a dialog moves to its next field.
- On Android, a crash report can hold a crashed page's own last lines, once adb has granted the app log access, and it names the sites in its log lines, never their whole addresses.
- On a Mac, installing an update from the app also clears its "ready" notification.
- On a Mac, after an update, macOS asks to let Project B use its key: choose Always Allow.
